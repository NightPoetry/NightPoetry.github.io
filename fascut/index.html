Privacy Policy

We take your privacy seriously. Here is how we handle your data.

1. Information We Collect

FasCut is a native macOS application. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data from the app itself. The app operates entirely on your device and requires no account or sign-in.

2. Feedback Form

When you voluntarily submit feedback through this website, we collect:

  • Name (optional) — used only to credit you if your suggestion is implemented
  • Email address — used solely to respond to your feedback or notify you about its resolution
  • Feedback content — used to improve the product

This data is never shared, sold, or used for marketing. It is retained only as long as needed to process your feedback.

3. Microphone Access

FasCut requests microphone permission solely for recording audio directly onto the timeline. All audio recordings are stored locally on your device. No audio data is ever transmitted externally.

4. Third-Party Services

FasCut does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking frameworks. Your activity within the app is never monitored or reported.

5. Children's Privacy

FasCut is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
